Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

Introduction

Wing & Bloom Conservatory ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit our garden, book a visit, or use this website, and how we use and protect it.

Information We Collect

  • Contact details you provide, such as your name, email address and phone number, when requesting a visit or contacting us
  • Visit preferences, such as preferred date, time, group size and visit type
  • Technical data such as browser type, device information and pages visited, collected automatically through cookies
  • Newsletter sign-up information, if you choose to subscribe

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to visit requests and manage bookings
  • To send updates, confirmations or reminders about your visit
  • To improve our website, exhibits and visitor experience
  • To send newsletters or promotional updates, only if you've opted in

Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies to remember your site preferences (such as dark mode and text direction) and to understand how visitors use our website. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though some features may not work as intended.

Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited data with trusted service providers who help us operate our website and manage bookings, and only when required by law.

Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. School and group visit bookings should be made by a parent, guardian or teacher on behalf of a group.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information at any time by contacting us using the details below.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. Changes will be posted on this page along with an updated revision date.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out via our Contact page.
