Butterfly Encounter

Wander among free-flying butterflies and experience the conservatory at its most magical.

DURATION Self-Paced
BEST FOR All Visitors
LOCATION Main Conservatory

OVERVIEW

Step Into A World Alive With Wings

Butterfly Encounter is the signature Wing & Bloom experience — an immersive journey through a tropical environment alive with free-flying butterflies, colourful flowers and lush foliage.

Instead of observing butterflies from behind glass, you enter their living environment. Walk along peaceful pathways while butterflies feed from flowers, glide above the garden and rest naturally among tropical leaves.

No two visits feel exactly the same. Butterfly activity changes throughout the day, creating quiet moments of discovery whenever you slow down and look carefully.

PLEASE NOTE

Visitor Guidelines

A few simple guidelines help keep both our butterflies and our visitors safe, calm and comfortable throughout the conservatory.

01

Dress Comfortably

The conservatory stays warm and humid year round, so light clothing and comfortable walking shoes are recommended.

02

Look, Don't Touch

Butterfly wings are extremely delicate. Please avoid touching or handling them and let them move freely on their own.

03

Photograph Gently

Personal photography is always welcome. Please avoid flash photography and give butterflies plenty of space.

04

Keep It Calm

Move slowly and keep your voice low so butterflies remain calm and undisturbed throughout your visit.
